# Investment Risk

Investment products can involve different levels and types of risk.

Customers should receive appropriate product information before investing.

## Key Concepts

- Capital may be at risk depending on the product.
- Historical performance does not guarantee future performance.
- Some products may have limited liquidity.
- Market conditions can affect investment values.
- Fees can reduce returns.

Customer-facing information must accurately describe the product and avoid presenting investments as guaranteed unless a guarantee genuinely applies.
