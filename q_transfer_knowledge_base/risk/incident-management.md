# Incident Management

An incident is an event that negatively affects customers, financial processing, security, availability, data, or business operations.

## Incident Severity

Severity may consider customer impact, financial impact, duration, number of affected users, regulatory implications, and security implications.

## Incident Process

Detect → classify → contain → investigate → resolve → communicate → document → review.

Major incidents require coordinated involvement from the relevant technical, operational, security, compliance, and management teams.
