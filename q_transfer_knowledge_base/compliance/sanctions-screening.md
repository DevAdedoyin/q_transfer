# Sanctions Screening

Q-Transfer screens relevant customers and transactions against applicable sanctions requirements.

## Screening Points

Screening may occur during onboarding, before or during transactions, and during ongoing customer monitoring.

## Potential Match

A potential match should be reviewed by authorised compliance personnel. Customer-facing employees must not independently clear a sanctions alert unless their role and procedure explicitly allow it.

## Escalation

Confirmed or unresolved high-risk matches must follow the company's sanctions escalation process.
