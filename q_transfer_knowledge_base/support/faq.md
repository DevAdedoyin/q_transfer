# Frequently Asked Questions

## Why is my transfer pending?

A transfer may remain pending because of processing, compliance review, payment partner processing, settlement, or another operational dependency.

## Why did my transfer fail?

A failed transfer can have several causes. The transaction record and relevant processing error should be reviewed before providing a specific explanation.

## Why is my verification taking longer?

Additional verification may be required because information is incomplete, automated checks require review, or the customer falls into a category requiring additional due diligence.

## Can customer support clear a compliance alert?

No. Compliance alerts must be handled by authorised personnel according to the applicable procedure.

## Are investment returns guaranteed?

Investment returns should not be described as guaranteed unless the specific product documentation confirms a genuine guarantee.
