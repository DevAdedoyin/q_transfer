# Q-Transfer Company Overview

Q-Transfer is a fictional fintech company used for a RAG engineering project. It provides local and international money transfers and investment services through mobile applications and selected branches.

## Business Profile

- Industry: Financial technology
- Core services: Local transfers, international transfers, investment products
- Workforce: 500+ employees
- Operating model: Digital-first with physical branches
- Customers: Individuals, small businesses, and selected corporate customers

## Core Departments

Q-Transfer operates through Engineering, Product, Operations, Customer Support, Compliance, Risk, Finance, Treasury, Human Resources, Legal, Information Security, Marketing, and Branch Operations.

## Customer Journey

A typical customer journey includes account registration, identity verification, funding, transaction initiation, compliance screening, transaction processing, settlement, notifications, and reconciliation.

## Important Principle

Customer funds and company operating funds must be treated separately. Financial transactions require appropriate controls, audit trails, reconciliation, and regulatory oversight.
