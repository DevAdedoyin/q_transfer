# Month-End Financial Close

Finance performs a controlled month-end close.

## Typical Activities

1. Reconcile bank and payment accounts.
2. Review transaction balances.
3. Record appropriate adjustments.
4. Review outstanding exceptions.
5. Validate investment-related balances where applicable.
6. Complete management and regulatory reporting activities.
7. Obtain required approvals.

Material unexplained differences should be escalated rather than concealed through unsupported adjustments.
