# Fees and Foreign Exchange

Q-Transfer may charge transfer fees and may apply an exchange-rate margin depending on the product and corridor.

## Customer Quote

A customer quote should clearly identify, where applicable:

- Amount sent
- Currency sent
- Exchange rate
- Transfer fee
- Amount received
- Currency received
- Quote expiry

## Example

A customer sends GBP 1,000 to a recipient receiving NGN. The final received amount depends on the applicable exchange rate, fees, and any relevant processing adjustments.

Rates should be sourced from approved systems rather than manually entered by customer support.
