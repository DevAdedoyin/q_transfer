# International Money Transfers

International transfers involve sending funds between supported countries and currencies.

## Typical Flow

1. Customer submits transfer.
2. Customer and transaction checks are performed.
3. Currency conversion and fees are calculated.
4. Compliance and sanctions screening occurs.
5. Payment instruction is created.
6. Partner or banking network processes the payment.
7. Settlement is confirmed.
8. Customer receives status updates.

## Important Considerations

International transfers may be affected by cut-off times, weekends, public holidays, correspondent or partner processing, compliance reviews, and currency availability.
