# Employee Roles and Access

Q-Transfer uses role-based access control.

## Examples

Customer Support employees can view appropriate customer account information needed to resolve support cases. They should not have unrestricted access to transaction administration tools.

Compliance analysts can review alerts, customer risk information, and transaction activity relevant to investigations.

Finance employees can access accounting and reconciliation information required for their duties.

Engineering employees should use controlled environments and should not access production customer data unless explicitly authorised for a documented operational need.

## Principle

Employees receive the minimum access required to perform their responsibilities. Access should be reviewed when an employee changes role or leaves the company.
