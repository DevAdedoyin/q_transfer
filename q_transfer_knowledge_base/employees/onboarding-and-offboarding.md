# Employee Onboarding and Offboarding

## Onboarding

New employees complete identity verification, employment documentation, security training, role-specific training, and access provisioning.

## Offboarding

When employment ends, access should be revoked promptly. Company equipment must be returned and relevant credentials, tokens, and privileged access must be disabled.

## Access Review

Managers are responsible for confirming that access matches the employee's current responsibilities. Privileged access requires additional controls and monitoring.
