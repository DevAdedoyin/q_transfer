# Customer Support

Customer Support handles account questions, transaction enquiries, verification issues, complaints, and service problems.

## Common Cases

- Transfer pending
- Transfer failed
- Recipient has not received funds
- Verification pending
- Account access issue
- Fee enquiry
- Exchange-rate enquiry
- Investment account question

## Escalation

Cases involving suspected fraud, account takeover, sanctions concerns, privacy incidents, or unexplained transaction activity must be escalated to the appropriate specialist team.

Support staff should not promise transaction reversals, refunds, or compliance outcomes without following the relevant procedure.
