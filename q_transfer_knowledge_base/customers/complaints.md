# Customer Complaints

A complaint is an expression of dissatisfaction about a Q-Transfer product, service, employee, process, or outcome.

## Complaint Process

1. Record the complaint.
2. Confirm receipt to the customer.
3. Categorise the issue.
4. Investigate using relevant records.
5. Escalate where required.
6. Provide an appropriate response.
7. Record the resolution.

Complaint records should contain enough information to support investigation and regulatory reporting where required.
