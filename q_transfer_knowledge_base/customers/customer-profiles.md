# Customer Profiles

Q-Transfer supports individual and business customers.

## Customer Lifecycle

1. Registration
2. Identity or business verification
3. Risk assessment
4. Account activation
5. Transaction activity
6. Ongoing monitoring
7. Account closure where applicable
