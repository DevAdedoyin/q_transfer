# Customer KYC and Verification

Know Your Customer procedures help Q-Transfer establish who its customers are and understand relevant risks.

## Individual Verification

Depending on jurisdiction and risk, verification may involve identity information, identity documents, address information, and other required checks.

## Business Verification

Business customers may require company registration information, ownership information, authorised representatives, business purpose, and other supporting evidence.

## Verification Outcomes

- Verified
- Pending review
- Additional information required
- Restricted
- Rejected

Higher-risk cases may require enhanced due diligence and additional review.
